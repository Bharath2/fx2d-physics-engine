# Support

## Getting help

- **Documentation.** The [documentation site](https://bharath2.github.io/fx2d-physics-engine/)
  has the guides, the API reference and the
  [Python guide](https://bharath2.github.io/fx2d-physics-engine/guides/python).
- **Questions, bugs and feature requests.** Open an
  [issue](https://github.com/Bharath2/fx2d-physics-engine/issues).

For a bug, the issue should give:

- the Fx2D version (`fx2d.__version__`, or the commit you built);
- your platform and compiler;
- the smallest scene that shows the problem, as YAML, Python or C++;
- what you expected, and what happened instead.

Fx2D is deterministic, so a scene and its inputs reproduce a problem exactly.

**Security problems.** Do not post the details in a public issue. Open an issue saying that you
have a security report, and a private channel will be arranged.

## Maintenance and governance

Fx2D is maintained by Bharath Chandra Irigireddy, on a best-effort basis with no guaranteed
response times. Decisions are discussed in issues and pull requests, and the maintainer makes
the final call.

Contributions are welcome. [CONTRIBUTING](./docs/contributing.md) describes the workflow, the
lint gate and the tests, and [CHANGELOG](./CHANGELOG.md) records each release.

Releases follow Semantic Versioning. While the version is 0.x, a minor version may break
compatibility, and every such change is listed in the changelog.
